Stock Prediction

A machine-learning project comparing classical and deep-learning approaches to forecasting stock prices: a statistical ARIMA baseline against a recurrent LSTM network. Built as a team for NUS's CS3244 (Machine Learning).

The question

Does a deep sequence model actually beat a well-tuned statistical baseline on noisy financial time series? ARIMA is a strong, interpretable classical forecaster; LSTMs can capture non-linear, longer-range structure. We put the two head to head on the same data to see where each wins.

Approach

  • Baseline — fit ARIMA on the price series, selecting differencing and lag orders, as an interpretable point of comparison.
  • Deep model — train an LSTM on windowed sequences to forecast forward prices.
  • Evaluation — compare the models' forecasts against held-out data to weigh accuracy against complexity and interpretability.
