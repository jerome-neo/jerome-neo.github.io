Mood tracking app: Moodal

App development

Team Members: Wei Jie Law, Rachelle Leong, Jerome Neo, Shaun Loong

Won 3rd place in a competition called Makerthon in early 2022. Competition was about improving the mental well-being of NUS students. We built a mood tracking app that also included improved accessibility to resources for mental health as well as proposed improvements to the current processes in which NUS students seek help at the University Health Centre (UHC).
